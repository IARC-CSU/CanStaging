<p>
    CNS involvement:
</p>
<p>
    · Any CNS tumour mass (identified by imaging techniques [ie, CT, MRI]); or
</p>
<p>
    · Cranial nerve palsy that cannot be explained by extradural lesions; or
</p>
<p>
    · Blasts morphologically identified in CSF. (In the absence of a CNS tumour
    mass and cranial nerve palsy, a CSF report is required to confirm or
    exclude CNS involvement.)
</p>
<p>
    BM involvement:
</p>
<p>
    Morphologic evidence of ≥ 5% blasts or lymphoma cells by BM aspiration or
    biopsy.
</p>
