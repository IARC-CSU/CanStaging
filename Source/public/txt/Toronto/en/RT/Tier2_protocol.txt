Two principal staging systems exist for renal tumours. Both systems are based on findings at surgery (except for stage IV which is based on presence of distant metastases at diagnosis).<br/>The COG/National Wilms Tumour Study Group (NWTSG) staging system is based on findings at surgery for patients who have not received chemotherapy prior to surgery. The SIOP staging system is based on findings at surgery for patients who have received chemotherapy prior to surgery.
