Distant metastasis disease (ie, not contiguous with the primary tumour), including metastasis to skin, liver or bone marrow.<br/>A clearly left sided abdominal tumour with right-sided lung, bone or pleura (or vice versa) involvement is defined as metastatic disease. Nonregional (distant) lymph node involvement is also metastatic disease.<br/>Ascites and/or a pleural effusion, even with malignant cells, do not constitute metastatic disease unless they are remote from the body compartment of the primary tumour.
