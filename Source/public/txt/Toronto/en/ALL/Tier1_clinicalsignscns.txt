<p>
    Clinical signs of CNS involvement are defined as
</p>
<p>
    - Cranial nerve palsy (e.g. facial weakness, ptosis), brain/eye
    involvement or hypothalamic syndrome.
</p>
<p>
    - Radiologic evidence of intracranial, intradural mass
</p>
<p>
    Extra-ocular orbital masses, severe headaches and eye swelling (in the
    absence of signs of cranial nerve involvement) are not sufficient to
    constitute CNS involvement.
</p>
