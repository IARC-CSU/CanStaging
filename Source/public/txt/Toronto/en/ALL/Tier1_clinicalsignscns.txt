Clinical signs of CNS involvement are defined as<br/>-	Radiologic evidence of intracranial, intradural mass<br/>-	Cranial nerve palsy (e.g. facial weakness, ptosis), brain/eye involvement or hypothalamic syndrome.<br/><br/>Extra-ocular orbital masses, severe headaches and eye swelling (in the absence of signs of cranial nerve involvement) are not sufficient to constitute CNS involvement.
